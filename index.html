Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Data collected

We collect the seller's phone number for authentication, name for the seller profile, and product photos, voice recordings, written descriptions and listing data when the seller submits them.

Purpose

We use this data only to authenticate the seller, create and store requested product listings, provide pricing assistance, and support sharing initiated by the seller.

Processors

Authentication may be processed by Firebase. Submitted photos, audio and descriptions may be sent to the configured AI provider. Hosting, database and storage providers process data to operate the service. Provider names and hosting locations must be confirmed before release.

Retention and security

Account data is retained while the account is active. Draft media and listings must be deleted after the configured retention period or when deletion is requested, except where retention is legally required. Production deployment must use encrypted transport, authenticated access and ownership checks.
